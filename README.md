# UPI Voice

Android app that speaks out every UPI payment received, like a shop soundbox.
Built for a Poco M3 (MIUI), works on any Android 8+ phone.

- **Auto-detects every UPI app on the phone** (anything that opens `upi://pay` links: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, PNB ONE, YONO SBI, apps installed later), plus bank credit SMS
- **Any amount**, from ₹1 upward with no upper limit, read the Indian way ("12 লাখ 34 হাজার 567 টাকা")
- **Instant**: the voice engine stays loaded, so the amount is spoken the moment the notification arrives
- Speaks in Bengali, Hindi or English ("500 টাকা পাওয়া গেছে")
- The same payment seen in the UPI app and the bank SMS is spoken once
- No internet permission, no ads, no tracking, no dependencies
